# Salla Authentication

## Overview

Salla is an e-commerce platform for building and running online stores (products, orders, customers, shipping, payments, and store management). The Salla API supports OAuth 2.0 so users can grant your app access to their Salla account data.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Salla's OAuth 2.0 to connect your Salla account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Salla Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Salla**
- You will be redirected to Salla's authorization page.
- Review and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Salla redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Salla account is now connected and ready to use.
