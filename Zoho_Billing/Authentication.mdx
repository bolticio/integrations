## Zoho Billing

### Overview
The ZohoBilling node allows you to connect your application with Zoho Billing (formerly Zoho Subscriptions) to automate tasks such as managing subscriptions, customers, invoices, payments, credit notes, plans, add-ons, and more. It helps streamline your subscription billing and recurring payment workflows by enabling seamless integration with your billing and subscription management data.

### Authentication Ways
- OAuth 2.0 [Zoho Documentation](https://www.zoho.com/billing/api/v1/oauth/)

### OAuth 2.0
To use the ZohoBilling node, you need to authenticate using OAuth 2.0. Follow the steps below to set up your OAuth credentials and authorize access to your Zoho Billing data.

**Steps:**

1. **Connect via Boltic**
   - In Boltic's integration configuration, select ZohoBilling and enter a Name for your connection.

2. **Authorize Access**
   - Click Connect or Authorize.
   - You will be redirected to the Zoho Accounts login and consent screen.
   - Sign in with your Zoho credentials and grant the requested scopes (e.g. `ZohoSubscriptions.creditnotes.CREATE`, `ZohoSubscriptions.customers.READ`, `ZohoSubscriptions.invoices.CREATE`, `ZohoSubscriptions.subscriptions.UPDATE`, `ZohoSubscriptions.payments.READ`, etc.).
   - Once the Connect button indicator turns green, return to Boltic and click Save — your OAuth tokens are now securely stored.

3. **Token Handling**
   - After successful authorization, Boltic securely stores both the access token and refresh token.
   - Access tokens are valid for 1 hour; when they expire, Boltic will automatically use the refresh token to obtain a new access token behind the scenes.

For detailed API usage (endpoints, request/response formats, supported modules and operations), see the [Zoho Billing API reference](https://www.zoho.com/billing/api/v1/introduction/)
