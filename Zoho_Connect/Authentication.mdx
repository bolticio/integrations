### Zoho Connect Authentication

Use OAuth2 to connect your Zoho Connect account.

Scopes requested:

- ZohoConnect.groups.ALL
- ZohoConnect.events.ALL
- ZohoConnect.tasks.ALL
- ZohoConnect.networks.ALL

Steps:

1. Create an OAuth client in Zoho API Console and note client_id, client_secret, and set redirect URI to the callback shown in the UI.
2. Click “Connect Zoho Connect Account” and complete the consent screen.
3. We’ll store refresh_token, access_token, and expiry automatically. You can disconnect anytime.
