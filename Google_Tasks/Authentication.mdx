# Google Tasks Authentication

## Overview

Google Tasks is a lightweight to do list application from Google that allows users to create, manage, and track tasks across devices.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Google OAuth 2.0 to connect your Google Tasks account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Google Tasks Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Google**
- You will be redirected to Google's authorization page.
- Review and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Google redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Google Tasks account is now connected and ready to use.
