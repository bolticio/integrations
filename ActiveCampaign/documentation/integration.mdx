# ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign is a customer experience automation platform that helps businesses manage contacts, email marketing campaigns, sales pipelines, and customer interactions.

## Available Resources

Here are some of the available resources you can use with the **ActiveCampaign** node:

### Resources

- **Contact**: A person stored in ActiveCampaign that can be used for marketing, sales, and customer communication.
- **Account**: A company or organization associated with contacts and sales opportunities.
- **Deal**: A sales opportunity tracked through different pipeline stages.

## Basic Operations

Here are some of the basic operations you can perform with the ActiveCampaign node:

### Contact

- **Create Contact**: Creates a new contact.
- **Delete Contact**: Deletes an Existing Contact.
- **Add Contact Note**: Adds Note to a Contact.
- **Add Contact to Automation**: Add Contact to an Existing Automation.
- **Update Contact Subscription**: Updates an Existing Contact Subscription.
- **Update Contact**: Updates an Existing Contact.


### Account

- **Create Account**: Creates a new Account in ActiveCampaign.
- **Add Contact To Account**: Add Contact To an Existing Account.

### Deal

- **Create Deal**: Creates a new Deal in ActiveCampaign.
- **Add Note To Deal**: Add Note To an Existing Deal.

## Conclusion

ActiveCampaign helps businesses automate marketing, manage customer relationships, track sales opportunities, and improve customer engagement through a unified platform.
