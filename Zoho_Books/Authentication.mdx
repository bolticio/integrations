## Zoho Books

### Overview

The ZohoBooks node allows you to connect your application with Zoho Books to automate tasks such as managing invoices, customers, vendors, items, expenses, payments, and more. It helps streamline your accounting and bookkeeping workflows by enabling seamless integration with your financial data.

### Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0 <a href="https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/oauth/" target="_blank">Zoho Documentation</a>

### OAuth 2.0

To use the ZohoBooks node, you need to authenticate using OAuth 2.0. Follow the steps below to set up your OAuth credentials and authorize access to your Zoho Books data.

**Steps:**

1. **Connect via Boltic**

   - In Boltic’s integration configuration, select **ZohoBooks** and enter a **Name** for your connection.

2. **Authorize Access**

   - Click **Connect** or **Authorize**.
   - You will be redirected to the Zoho Accounts login and consent screen.
   - Sign in with your Zoho credentials and grant the requested scopes (e.g. `ZohoBooks.invoices.READ`, `ZohoBooks.customers.CREATE`, `ZohoBooks.expenses.READ`, etc.).
   - Once the **Connect** button indicator turns green, return to Boltic and click **Save** — your OAuth tokens are now securely stored.

3. **Token Handling**

   - After successful authorization, Boltic securely stores both the **access token** and **refresh token**.
   - Access tokens are valid for 1 hour; when they expire, Boltic will automatically use the refresh token to obtain a new access token behind the scenes.

For detailed API usage (endpoints, request/response formats, supported modules and operations), see the <a href="https://www.zoho.com/books/api/v3/introduction/" target="_blank">Zoho Books API reference</a>
