# Fathom Authentication

## Overview

Fathom is a meeting intelligence platform that records calls, generates summaries, and provides searchable transcripts. The Fathom API supports OAuth 2.0 so users can grant your app access to their Fathom account data.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Fathom's OAuth 2.0 to connect your Fathom account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Fathom Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Fathom**
- You will be redirected to Fathom's authorization page.
- Review and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Fathom redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Fathom account is now connected and ready to use.
