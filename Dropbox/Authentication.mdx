# Dropbox Authentication

## Overview

Dropbox is used to store, sync, and share files securely across devices and with others.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Dropbox OAuth 2.0 to connect your Dropbox account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Dropbox Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Dropbox**
- You will be redirected to Dropbox authorization page.
- Review and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Dropbox redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Dropbox account is now connected and ready to use.
