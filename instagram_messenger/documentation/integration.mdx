# Instagram Messenger

Instagram Messenger is a messaging platform for direct business communication. The Instagram Messenger node in Boltic lets you connect your Instagram Messenger business account and automate messaging actions using supported resources and operations.

## Instagram Messenger Account

- Select the Instagram Messenger account you want to connect.
- If you do not have an existing account, add one using the **Add New Account** option before proceeding.

## Available Resources

Here are the available resources you can use with the **Instagram Messenger** node:

### Resources
- **Message**: Work with Instagram Messenger direct messaging by selecting existing conversation recipients and sending text messages from your connected Instagram Messenger business account.

## Basic Operations

Here are the basic operations you can perform with the Instagram Messenger node:

### Message
- **Send Text Message**: Send a direct message to a user from an existing Instagram Messenger conversation.

## Notes

- You can only message users who already have an existing conversation with your connected Instagram Messenger account.
- Instagram Messenger availability is subject to Meta platform rules and messaging windows.

## Conclusion

Instagram Messenger helps you automate business messaging inside Boltic. Use the Instagram Messenger node to connect your account, select an existing conversation recipient, and send direct messages in your workflows.
