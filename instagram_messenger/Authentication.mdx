# Instagram Messenger Authentication

## Overview

Instagram Messenger is a messaging platform for direct business communication, and this integration lets you connect Instagram Messenger with Boltic. This integration uses OAuth 2.0 so users can grant Boltic access to their Instagram Messenger professional account.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Instagram Messenger OAuth 2.0 to connect your Instagram Messenger account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Instagram Messenger Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Instagram**
- You will be redirected to Instagram's authorization page.
- Sign in if needed and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Instagram redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Instagram Messenger account is now connected and ready to use.
