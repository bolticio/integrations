# Zoho Desk

## Overview
Zoho Desk is a cloud-based customer service software that helps manage customer support tickets, automate workflows, and streamline communication between teams and clients. It provides a comprehensive API that allows developers to integrate ticketing, contacts, tasks, and knowledge base functionalities into their own systems.  
Access to the Zoho Desk API is secured via **OAuth 2.0** authentication to ensure authorized and auditable access.

---

## Authentication

The Zoho Desk integration uses **OAuth 2.0** for secure and seamless authentication.

### 🔹 To connect your Zoho Desk account:

1. Click **Connect Zoho Desk** on Boltic.  
2. A Zoho authorization page will open — log in with your Zoho account.  
3. Review and **allow all requested permissions**.  
4. Once approved, Boltic will automatically retrieve your access and refresh tokens securely.  

That’s it! You’re now connected to Zoho Desk and can start building automations instantly.
