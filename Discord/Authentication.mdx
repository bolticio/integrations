# Discord

## Overview

Discord is a communication platform for communities, voice, video, and text. The Discord API supports OAuth 2.0 so users can grant your app access to their Discord account data.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Discord's OAuth 2.0 to connect your Discord account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Discord Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Discord**
- You will be redirected to Discord's authorization page.
- Select the server you want to connect.
- Review and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Discord redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Discord account is now connected and ready to use.
