# Pipedrive

Pipedrive is a sales CRM platform that helps teams manage leads, deals, contacts, organizations, and activities. The Pipedrive node in Boltic lets you connect to your Pipedrive data to sync entities, power analytics, and automate workflows through a comprehensive set of operations designed for seamless sales process management.

## Pipedrive Account

- Select the Pipedrive account you want to connect.
- If you don't have an existing account, please add one using the "Add New Account" option before proceeding.

## Available Resources

Here are some of the available resources you can use with the **Pipedrive** node:

### Resources
- **Activities**: Manage and track calls, meetings, and tasks linked to deals, leads, persons, organizations, or projects.
- **Users**: Access company users, their roles, permissions, settings, and profile information.
- **Leads**: Capture and qualify potential opportunities before converting them into deals.
- **CallLogs**: Store and retrieve records of calls, including outcomes and associations to CRM items.
- **Deals**: Manage sales opportunities in pipelines, including participants, products, discounts, installments, and activities.
- **Mailbox**: Work with connected email threads and messages linked to CRM items.
- **Notes**: Create and manage notes and comments attached to deals, persons, and organizations.
- **Organizations**: Manage company accounts, relationships, files, permitted users, and communication history.
- **Persons**: Manage individual contacts and their linked deals, products, files, and communications.
- **Pipelines**: Define and manage sales pipelines, track movement, and analyze conversion performance.
- **Products**: Maintain your product catalog, variations, prices, and their associations to deals and contacts.
- **Projects**: Plan and track post-sale work with groups, phases, boards, tasks, and activities.
- **Roles**: Configure role definitions, assignments, and visibility settings to control access.
- **Stages**: Define pipeline stages and update their properties to reflect your sales process.
- **Tasks**: Create and manage project tasks and updates within Projects.
