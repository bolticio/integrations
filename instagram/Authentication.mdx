# Instagram Authentication

## Overview

Instagram is a social media platform for sharing photos, videos, reels, comments, and direct messages, and this integration lets you connect Instagram business messaging with Boltic. This integration uses OAuth 2.0 so users can grant Boltic access to their Instagram professional account.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Instagram OAuth 2.0 to connect your Instagram account to Boltic.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Instagram Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Instagram**
- You will be redirected to Instagram's authorization page.
- Sign in if needed and approve the requested permissions.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Instagram redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Instagram account is now connected and ready to use.
