# Instagram Integration

Instagram is a social media platform for sharing photos, videos, reels, comments, and direct messages. The Instagram node in Boltic lets you connect your Instagram business account and automate messaging actions using supported Instagram resources and operations.

## Instagram Account

- Select the Instagram account you want to connect.
- If you do not have an existing account, add one using the **Add New Account** option before proceeding.

## Available Resources

Here are the available resources you can use with the **Instagram** node:

### Resources
- **Message**: Work with Instagram direct messaging by selecting existing conversation recipients and sending text messages from your connected Instagram business account.

## Basic Operations

Here are the basic operations you can perform with the Instagram node:

### Message
- **Send Text Message**: Send a direct message to a user from an existing Instagram conversation.

## Notes

- You can only message users who already have an existing conversation with your connected Instagram account.
- Instagram messaging availability is subject to Meta platform rules and messaging windows.

## Conclusion

Instagram helps you automate business messaging inside Boltic. Use the Instagram node to connect your account, select an existing conversation recipient, and send direct messages in your workflows.
