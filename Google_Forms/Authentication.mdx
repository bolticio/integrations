# Google Forms Authentication

## Overview

Google Forms is a form builder from Google that allows users to create forms, collect responses, and work with response data in Google Sheets.

## Authentication Ways

- OAuth 2.0

## OAuth 2.0

Use Google OAuth 2.0 to connect your Google Forms account to Boltic.

This connection requests access to manage Google Forms, read form responses, access Google Drive files used by the integration, work with Google Sheets response data, and identify the connected Google account.

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Click Connect**
- In Boltic, click **Connect Google Forms Account**.

**Step 2: Authorize in Google**
- You will be redirected to Google's authorization page.
- Review and approve the requested permissions for Google Forms, form responses, Google Drive, Google Sheets, and your Google profile.

**Step 3: Return to Boltic**
- After approval, Google redirects you back to Boltic.
- Your Google Forms account is now connected and ready to use.
